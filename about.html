About Our Farm

Learn more about Eric's Poultry Farm in Nakuru

Who We Are

Eric's Poultry Farm is a modern poultry business located in Nakuru, Kenya. We are dedicated to producing high-quality poultry products including broiler chickens, layer chickens, day-old chicks, and fresh eggs.

Our farm was established with a vision to improve food security and support farmers with reliable poultry solutions. We focus on hygiene, proper feeding, and modern farming techniques to ensure healthy production.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality poultry products and empower farmers with sustainable poultry farming knowledge.

Our Vision

To become the leading poultry supplier in Nakuru and beyond, delivering fresh and healthy poultry products.

Our Values

Quality, Integrity, Hygiene, and Customer Satisfaction.
